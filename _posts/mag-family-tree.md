# Spacecraft Missions

> **Authors Note:** Boom lengths are hand-wavey! In some cases, the reported value is the approximate distance between the magnetometer sensor and the spacecraft center rather than the physical boom length.

| Mission | Target / environment | Launch year | Number and type | Boom length / location |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Luna 1 [^a] | Lunar flyby / heliocentric | 1959 | 1 | Spacecraft-mounted |
| Luna 10 [^b] | Lunar orbit | 1966 | 1 | Spacecraft-mounted |
| Explorer 35 [^c] | Lunar orbit | 1967 | 2 | 2.2 m |
| Pioneer 10 [^d] | Jupiter / interplanetary | 1972 | 1 HVM | \(\sim 6.5\) m |
| Pioneer 11 [^d] | Jupiter and Saturn | 1973 | 1 HVM + 1 FGM | \(\sim 6.5\) m |
| Voyager 1/2 [^e] | Outer planets / heliosphere | 1977 | 4: 2 LFM + 2 HFM | 13 m |
| AMPTE-CCE [^f] | Geospace | 1984 | 2 | 2.3 m |
| AMPTE-IRM [^g] | Geospace | 1984 | 1 | 2 m |
| AMPTE-UKS [^h] | Geospace | 1984 | 1 | 1 m |
| Giotto [^i] | Comet Halley / interplanetary | 1985 | 2 | Spacecraft-mounted |
| Galileo [^j] | Jupiter system | 1989 | 2 | 11 m |
| Ulysses [^k] | Solar polar orbit | 1990 | 2 | 5.6 m |
| Mars Global Surveyor [^l] | Mars orbit | 1996 | 2 | Solar-array tips; \(\sim 2\)–\(2.5\) m |
| NEAR Shoemaker [^m] | Near-Earth asteroid Eros | 1996 | 1 | \(\sim 1\) m |
| ACE [^n] | Sun–Earth L1 | 1997 | 2 triaxial fluxgate | 4.19 m from spacecraft center |
| Cassini [^o] | Saturn system | 1997 | 2: FGM + V/SHM | 11 m |
| Lunar Prospector [^p] | Lunar orbit | 1998 | 1 | 0.8 m; \(\sim 2.6\) m total structure |
| MESSENGER [^q] | Mercury orbit | 2004 | 1 | 3.6 m |
| THEMIS [^r] | Geospace | 2007 | 2: FGM + SCM | 1.2 m FGM; 1 m SCM |
| SELENE/Kaguya [^s] | Lunar orbit | 2007 | 1 | 12 m |
| Juno [^t] | Jupiter orbit | 2011 | 2 | \(\sim 10\)–\(12\) m solar-array boom |
| MAVEN [^u] | Mars orbit | 2013 | 2 triaxial fluxgate | 0.66 m boomlets; sensors \(\sim 5.6\) m from center |
| Swarm [^v] | Earth orbit | 2013 | 3: 2 vector + 1 scalar | 4.3 m |
| BepiColombo/MPO-MAG [^w] | Mercury orbit | 2018 | 2 | 2.8–2.9 m |
| BepiColombo/Mio-MGF [^x] | Mercury orbit | 2018 | 2 | 4.4 m |
| Parker Solar Probe [^y] | Solar corona / heliosphere | 2018 | 3 magnetic sensors | 3.5 m |
| Solar Orbiter [^z] | Solar orbit | 2020 | 2 fluxgate sensors | 4.4 m |
| KPLO/Danuri [^aa] | Lunar orbit | 2022 | 3 | 1.2 m |
| JUICE [^ab] | Jupiter system | 2023 | 3: 2 fluxgate + 1 scalar | 10.6 m |
| Europa Clipper [^ac] | Jovian system / Europa | 2024 | 3 fluxgate sensors | 7.9–8.5 m |
